\textbf{Differentiable Monte Carlo (MC) ray tracers.}
Sionna RT~\cite{sionna} differentiates antenna patterns and scattering coefficients but suspends gradients through its path solver and does not model wideband FMCW. Hofmann et al.~\cite{hofmann2025inverse} estimate per-vertex stepped-frequency materials but assume known geometry and single-bounce propagation. InverTwin~\cite{chen2025invertwin} addresses multipath gradient pathologies via path-space differentiation, but uses a Gaussian-kernel surrogate for range processing and validates on synthetic data only. mmIR~\cite{mmIR2026} is the closest prior work and the only fair MC comparison: a differentiable ray tracer with per-vertex ITU materials, normals, and antenna patterns on real captures. This family is physically faithful and complex-valued but not multi-viewpoint-tractable. mmIR's ${\sim}20$~min per single-viewpoint fit at 24k surface interactions does not scale to the multi-view optimization NVS demands. 3DPS preserves the physical fidelity and complex output of MC ray tracers while replacing per-iteration MC sampling with deterministic PSF splatting at constant cost per (point, range bin), yielding ${\sim}45\times$ per-scene speedup over mmIR.

\textbf{Optical-NVS adaptations.}
DART~\cite{huang2024dart} trains a NeRF on range--Doppler magnitude. Radar Fields~\cite{10.1145/3641519.3657510} synthesizes power-only RA via a hash-grid + MLP. SpINR~\cite{takawale2025spinr} models FMCW occupancy implicitly. RadarSplat~\cite{kung2025radarsplat} ports 3D Gaussian Splatting~\cite{3dgs} to RA rendering with opaque per-Gaussian features that predict radar intensity, providing no physics-based material model, no complex output, and no mechanism to separate scene properties from sensor-specific artifacts. RF-4D~\cite{zhang2025rf4d}, NeuRadar~\cite{rafidashti2025neuradar}, and NeWRF~\cite{lu2024newrf} extend to dynamic scenes or fuse radar with RGB/LiDAR but render no complex output. This family is multi-viewpoint-tractable but neither physically faithful nor complex-valued: every method's supervision is on post-FFT power-only RA images, phase is dropped by construction, and the radar's signal-model components (antenna patterns, sidelobes, FFT windowing, materials) are absorbed into opaque learned features rather than rendered explicitly. 3DPS preserves the multi-view tractability of these methods while adding explicit ITU-R~P.2040 BSDF material modeling, real antenna beam patterns, and per-(TX, RX) complex range profile output.
